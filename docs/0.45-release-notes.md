# Sandcastle 0.45 — "Nothing Was Broken"

> **Draft.** Workstream A (ACP client) has not landed yet. Everything below is
> implemented and tested; the ACP section is missing and is marked at the end.

## Nothing was broken. That was the problem.

A gate that rejects and lets the work through isn't a gate. It's a formality.
It files the report anyway. It runs the payment anyway. And it tells you, in
writing, that everything completed.

We shipped 83 templates with gates in them. A sanctions screener. A payment
run. A discount approval. Not one of them could stop anything — the engine
judged, wrote down "rejected," and walked past its own guard rail. Zero of 83
ever read the verdict.

You didn't get a warning. You got a green check.

**This release is about the things that failed quietly.**

---

## The gate now stops the run

`fail_on_reject` defaults to true. A rejection fails the step, and it isn't
retryable — re-running a judge until it says yes turns a guard rail into a dice
roll.

Two of our own templates had been rejecting **100% of the time** and nobody
could tell, because rejection did nothing. Their prompts asked the judge for
"PASS." The engine was listening for "approved." We scanned all 116 judge
prompts. Those were the only two. They're fixed.

Not every gate is a guard. 32 of 88 are filters — "no material deals today" is a
Tuesday, not a failure. Those are marked, individually, with a stated reason.

## Replay stopped re-sending your POST

`--replay` re-spent tokens and re-sent requests. The memoizer was real, but it
lived on a code path that 24 of 25 step types never reach — `llm` and `http`
among them. One line in the source admitted it:
`REPLAY_SAFE_STEP_TYPES = frozenset({"standard"})`.

Now there's a durable effect ledger. The claim, and we tested it without the
shortcut that would have made it pass by accident:

> **Two full runs. One POST. $0 the second time.**

When the ledger can't be reached, the answer follows the deployment instead of a
flag. Your laptop has no database, so it runs and warns. A server does, so it
stops. The safe default is the one most people actually run.

## A memory scope could contain `..`

`workflow:..`, `agent:..`, `tenant:../workflow:x` — all accepted. Harmless
against a vector store. A path-traversal primitive the moment anything writes to
disk. The pattern also ended in `$`, so a trailing newline slipped through.

Fixed, with the backend seam that makes the next release's markdown vault
possible.

---

## Breaking

Read this part.

- **A gate that rejects now fails the run.** Using one as a filter? Set
  `fail_on_reject: false`.
- **A failed step publishes no output.** The verdict is in `.error`, not
  `{steps.gate.decision}`.
- **`MEMORY_BACKEND=cloud` used to silently give you `local`.** Now it errors.
  Loud is better.

## Not in this release

Crash-resume. Fan-out sub-step checkpoints. `classify` stays outside the ledger —
memoizing it would silently un-skip the wrong branch.

Five connectors still don't work. That isn't fixed here, and it shouldn't
survive 0.46.
